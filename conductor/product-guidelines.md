# Product Guidelines: Leafcloud App

## Voice and Tone
- **Professional & Technical:** Communication should be precise, direct, and authoritative, suitable for infrastructure management.

## Visual Identity
- **Minimalist & Modern:** Clean design with high-contrast elements and ample white space.
- **Eco-Focused & Sustainable:** Visual cues (such as green highlights or carbon metrics) to emphasize the project's sustainability goals.
- **Infrastructure-Centric (NOC):** A default dark theme with high-visibility neon indicators for system health.

## UX Principles
- **Urgency-Driven Navigation:** Critical alerts and system health status are prioritized and front-and-center.
- **Data Clarity & Insights:** Complex metrics are simplified through intuitive data visualizations and graphs.
- **Mobile-First Interaction:** Optimized for mobile devices with smooth transitions, haptic feedback, and a responsive layout.

## Accessibility and Inclusivity
- **Standard WCAG Compliance:** Full support for screen readers, high-contrast modes, and dynamic text sizes.
- **Simplified Interaction Patterns:** Large, legible fonts and generous tap targets for ease of use.
- **Localized Experience:** Multi-language support to accommodate a global user base.
